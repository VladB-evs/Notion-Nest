Notion Nest

Free widgets for Notion

Pick a widget, copy its embed link, and paste it into a Notion page with /embed. No accounts, no sign-in, nothing to install.

Widgets

Year Progress
A grid of dots, one per day of the year. Past days fill in black, today glows red, the rest stay gray. Updates itself automatically at midnight.
Life & Time
One-line stat pills: a live birthday count-up, how far through today you are, a Christmas countdown, and a date counter for any event — days until, or days since. Each is a rounded card sized to fill a single line.
Flip Clock
A live split-flap clock in your own timezone. Digits flip over as time passes, just like the airport boards. Choose 24-hour or AM/PM, with or without seconds.
Season Progress
How far through the current season you are, on a soft gradient card that matches the season — no editing needed. It figures out the season on its own and switches over automatically when the next one begins.
