NotionNest

Widgets for Notion,
black & white.

Pick a widget, copy its embed link, and paste it into a Notion page with /embed. No accounts, no sign-in, nothing to install.

Widgets

Year Progress
A grid of dots, one per day of the year. Past days fill in black, today glows red, the rest stay gray. Updates itself automatically at midnight.
Life & Time
Small, one-line stats: a live birthday count-up, how far through today you are, and a Christmas countdown. Solid dark or light card background — Notion's own embed frame isn't theme-aware, so a transparent background would just show white; this always looks intentional instead.
